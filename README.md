## 🚀 About Me
I'm a Data Scientist, Software Developer, and IT Consultant based in Yaoundé, Cameroon. I hold dual Master's degrees in Data Science (University of Yaoundé I) and Transport Planning (ENSTP, ULB, Padua, Sapienza).

- 🔭 I’m currently working on **AWók**, a crowdsourcing platform for Ewondo speech recognition/synthesis, and researching **Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS)**.
- 🌱 I’m currently exploring personalized modeling of urban walking dynamics and advanced machine learning for African languages.
- 📝 I recently co-authored a paper on pedestrian safety in *Future Transportation* and presented at the DASSA 2025 workshop.
- 💬 Ask me about: Machine Learning (ASR, TTS, CNNs), urban mobility analysis, and web/mobile development.
